Privacy Policy for Typlo

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Welcome to Typlo ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (Typlo - Hindi & English Typing Tutor & CPCT Exam Simulator).

1. Information We Collect

We strive to collect minimal information necessary to provide and improve our typing tutor and exam simulation services:

  • Local Typing Data & Statistics: Your typing speed (WPM), accuracy, completed lessons, exam scores, and practice history are stored locally on your device.
  • Account & Authentication Information: If you choose to sign in or use cloud-backed features (such as syncing progress or leaderboards), we may collect basic account details (such as your name and email address) securely via Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • In-App Purchases & Monetization: Purchases made within the app (such as Typlo Pro upgrades) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or process your credit card or financial details on our servers.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: We may collect anonymous crash reports and performance diagnostics to improve app stability and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for:

  • Providing, maintaining, and personalizing your typing lessons and exam simulations.
  • Tracking your learning progress, speed statistics, and generating completion certificates.
  • Processing in-app purchases and managing your Typlo Pro subscription status.
  • Fixing bugs and improving app performance.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Local data remains securely stored on your device's private storage sandbox.

4. Third-Party Services

Typlo integrates with trusted third-party services that adhere to strict privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services & Billing: For app distribution, updates, and secure payment processing.
  • Firebase (Google): For optional authentication and cloud data synchronization.

5. Children's Privacy

Typlo is suitable for users of all ages learning typing skills. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
